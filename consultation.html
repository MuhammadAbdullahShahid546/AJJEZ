Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our growth consultation sessions.

Who is this consultation designed for?

This consultation is built for founders, marketing leads, and e-commerce/SaaS brands who are actively running or preparing to run paid media campaigns and want actionable, non-fluff guidance to scale ROAS and lower customer acquisition costs.

What happens during the 45-minute audit call?

We review your live dashboards (or screenshots), unpack your highest-friction drop-offs, dissect your current creative angles, and formulate a customized 30-day testing plan. No generic slide decks; only raw, tactical insights.

How is this different from hiring a regular digital agency?

Agencies often pass your account to junior media buyers while charging high management fees. With AJJEZ, you work directly with Muhammad Abdullah Shahid, the founder of AJJEZ, who focuses strictly on unit economics and sustainable revenue growth.
